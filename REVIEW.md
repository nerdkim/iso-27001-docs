# Full body review: 2026-09-17

> 한국어: [REVIEW.ko.md](REVIEW.ko.md)

All **93 Korean/English control pairs (186 documents)** were read in full and compared.
Corrections were applied to **73 pairs (146 documents)**. The other **20 pairs** had no substantive
finding in this pass. These counts describe controls, not the number of individual defects.

## 1. Scope and method

The review covered metadata and all six sections of every control: meaning across languages,
technical accuracy, unsupported universal claims, internal consistency, and whether evidence and
nonconformity examples matched the stated safeguards. It also checked related-control references
and retained the source/limitation footer. This was a direct reading of every body, followed by
paired corrections and automated verification, not a conclusion inferred from passing checks.

The most substantial corrections concerned credentials, deletion and retention, incident reporting,
BYOD and offboarding, physical safety, recovery capability, test data, and deployment decisions.
Appropriate alternatives and accountable exceptions were made explicit where the old wording
treated one implementation choice as universally mandatory. Exceptions do not override applicable
law or other binding obligations. Existing Codex setup and checker changes were preserved.

No licensed ISO text was used for an authoritative comparison. Main-body clauses, formal control
attributes, 2013 equivalence, and ISMS-P mapping sufficiency were not independently certified.
Crosswalks remain reference aids. A row with no finding is a review outcome, not a guarantee of
completeness or certification compliance. The explanations remain original project material.
